Coefficient lists are highest-degree-first, finite, native beta-coded prefixes. Leading-zero padding is distinct from convolution's right-zero extension. Its remainder is empty or has the proved strict represented-degree bound. Execution uniqueness compares decoded coefficient prefixes and retained lengths. It does not claim uniqueness for every arbitrary identity witness, and it does not by itself establish polynomial gcd, Bezout, or the full G091 milestone.
