## NG001 and SN002

NG001 proves the closed HA statement

```text
forall a b. ~(a=b) -> exists k. (a+S k=b \/ b+S k=a)
```

Its actual source is the dependency-free `lt_trichotomy` body from node48
of the immutable v28 artifact. PA4 and a freshly checked addition-commutativity
proof transport the witness to this exact orientation. The existing `Lt`
abbreviation has a different syntactic orientation; it is not silently
substituted for this formula. Z3 independently confirmed the conjecture;
its output is not an HA axiom or a reconstructed proof log.

SN002 reuses the exact ND0157 relation, writing `Square(p,n,s)` here only
as explanatory notation for `SignedDifferenceSquare(p,n,s)`:

```text
forall ap an bp bn s t.
  Square(ap,an,s) -> Square(bp,bn,t) ->
  ~(ap=an /\ bp=bn) ->
  exists k. (s+S k=2*t \/ 2*t+S k=s)
```

If `s=2*t`, the checked SN001 theorem forces both signed differences to
vanish, contradicting the supplied nonzero-pair premise. NG001 then returns
the positive gap. The bundle includes the complete 206-node SN001 proof,
the three-node NG001 proof, and a new root with dependencies `(205,208)`.
Nothing depends on normalization of the signed representatives.

This closes the **integer-discreteness component** of IR032, not its
real-algebraic lower bound. In particular, no conjugate-height bound or
rational-denominator clearing is hidden inside `Square`.

## SN003

Write the represented integers as `A=ap-an`, `B=bp-bn`, `C=cp-cn`, and
`D=dp-dn`. Two separate balance premises require the output pairs to
represent `R=AC+2BD` and `T=AD+BC`. Their squares, and the four input squares,
are explicit natural witnesses `r,t,a,b,c,d`. The conclusion is

```text
r+2*(a*d+b*c) = (a*c+(2*2)*(b*d))+2*t
```

This is exactly `(R²-2T²)=(A²-2B²)(C²-2D²)`, expressed without subtraction
in the original HA signature. All eighteen variables are universally
quantified; the six square premises and two output balances remain separate.
The new local aliases ND0388 `IQuadProductReal` and ND0389
`IQuadProductRadical` are conservative abbreviations for those two balance
equations. They do not supply a norm law or multiplication-existence theorem.

The proof reuses the actual v28 bodies for signed-square transport (693),
products (697), sum compensation (700), cross interchange (708), scaling
(715), and cancellative cross-sum transport (272), with their complete
33-node ancestor union. Three small closed identities are generated by the
existing deterministic ring procedure, with at most four variables. Large
compound terms are then substituted into these universal lemmas; the entire
eighteen-variable target is never sent to a monolithic ring normalizer.

The mathematical cancellation is:

```text
r+4N = U+4P
t+2N = V+2P
therefore r+2V = U+2t
```

Here `U=ac+4bd`, `V=ad+bc`, and `P,N` are the positive and negative parts
of the common cross product. Actual node693 transports the squares to
arbitrary supplied output representatives. Gaussian `GNorm=A²+B²` is not
used as a substitute for `A²-2B²`.
