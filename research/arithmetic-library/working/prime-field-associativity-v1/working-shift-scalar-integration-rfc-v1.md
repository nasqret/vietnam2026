## Mathematical scope

Coefficients are highest-degree first. The shift graph preserves the actual
decoded prefix and appends an actual zero; unlike left padding it represents
multiplication by X. Nonempty covariance gives the successor product length.
Empty factors require the separately proved zero/formal-equivalence cases;
no universal successor-length equation is assumed.

Scalar covariance uses the existing `FpPolyScale` graph, including its scalar
bound even for an empty prefix. Polynomial covariance works for nonzero
moduli and canonical scalars; primality is not silently added. Equality is
decoded-prefix or all-power formal coefficient equivalence, never equality
of raw beta encodings or merely equality of evaluations.
