## Mathematical boundary

Representations use highest-degree-first beta-coded coefficient prefixes.
Left padding means genuine leading zeros followed by the original decoded
prefix; it is not the convolution machinery's right-zero extension. Formal
equivalence compares coefficients at each natural power, not evaluations at
field elements, and never equates arbitrary code numbers.

The division execution independently computes quotient coefficients from
actual prior convolution coefficients, subtraction and multiplication by
the inverse of the divisor's actual nonzero head. Product tables, residual
tables and leading-zero trims are constructed. The strict remainder-degree
alternative and actual coefficient identity are proved separately; they are
not inserted as conclusion-bearing execution premises. Empty input,
constant divisors, arbitrary leading zeros and characteristic two retain
their stated guards. No represented natural degree is assigned to zero.

Distributivity concerns actual finite coefficient convolutions and their
actual modular sum witnesses. It is not inferred from equality of polynomial
evaluations. Both genuine product constructors are outputs of theorems.

These rows alone do not assert a completed polynomial gcd/Bézout algorithm,
irreducible-polynomial existence in every positive degree, or the general
prime-power-field endpoint G091.
