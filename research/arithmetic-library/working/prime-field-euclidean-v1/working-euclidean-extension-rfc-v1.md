## Additional mathematical scope

Execution uniqueness concerns the actual triangular quotient recursion,
actual residual convolution/subtraction and actual leading-zero trim. It
identifies quotient/remainder representation lengths and decoded coefficient
prefixes. It does not identify beta codes, constrain unused coefficients, or
claim uniqueness of arbitrary identity witnesses at unrelated lengths.
Conditional functionality does not assume primality; the existence theorem
uses the actual prime-field/nonzero-leading-divisor hypotheses.

Convolution padding is genuine leading-zero padding in either factor, not
right-zero convolution extension, evaluation equality, or unit association.
For nonempty factors it proves the actual shifted product length and decoded
leading-zero output padding. Empty factors are handled through actual zero
products and formal coefficient-at-power equivalence, without asserting a
false unconditional product-length shift.

Both-factor compatibility assumes p is nonzero and two actual products. It
constructs an actual intermediate product and composes the proved one-factor
compatibilities. The separate prime-field construction theorem produces a
genuine padded product and its formal equivalence; an output product or its
identity is not assumed.
