## Exact constructive flagship roots

The reviewed proof campaigns, in canonical ownership order, are:

1. **Lucas:** the complete unrestricted multidigit theorem `lucas_theorem`.
2. **Kummer:** both exact endpoints `kummer_binomial_carry_bit_count` and
   `kummer_carry_free_iff_not_divides`.
3. **Bertrand:** the strict constructive prime-existence theorem
   `bertrand_strict`.
4. **Four squares:** the unconditional universal Lagrange theorem
   `four_square_lagrange`.
5. **Two squares:** the complete zero-inclusive all-natural classification
   `two_square_iff_zero_or_even_three_mod_four_prime_valuations`.

Their exact joint dependency union contains **1,113 distinct theorem nodes**.
Precisely **673** of these were `body_checked` in Alpha v17. Canonical bundle
ownership, taking the first containing artifact in the order above, divides
those new evidence transitions into **74 Lucas, 73 Kummer, 241 Bertrand, 196
four-square, and 89 two-square** rows.

The newline-separated immutable ordered promotion names have SHA-256
`5b6faad95b90a3b3f11e6aea929aefd3cdbf9b5a1f3563e57d8e48f15e9d59e6`.
Only these exact rows may transition from `body_checked` to `alpha_closed`.
All other rows, including 17 K3C obligations and 67 auxiliary Bertrand rows,
remain byte-exact and unavailable for checked use.

## Genuine proof evidence

Every campaign has a separately canonical, self-contained constructive proof
bundle. Each local node contains its full exact ordinary dependency-curried
proof body, its exact closed formula, and all local dependency edges.

The original intuitionistic Python kernel independently checks every proof
body against its exact curried formula, while the separately compiled Lean
proof-bundle verifier independently validates the complete artifact. Lucas,
both Kummer endpoints, Bertrand, Lagrange, and the all-natural two-square
endpoint also have explicitly reconstructed ordinary empty-context
certificates checked by the original kernel. Every complete graph satisfies
the independent compiled-Lean gate before any Alpha-v18 artifacts are written.

All newly reconstructed proof bodies obey the unchanged original bounds of
**16 bodies, 125,000 structural proof nodes, and 25,000 proof objects per
microbatch**. Bundle reuse does not grant theorem authority: every selected
artifact is decoded and fully checked before any newly promoted theorem is
compiled into an ordinary empty-context proof and independently kernel checked.
No unchecked theorem name, content digest, host computation, classical rule,
external axiom, or `sorry` is trusted.

One Bertrand proof body requires an explicitly disclosed, proof-only
normalization: `pow_two_seed_bundle_from_total` retains its exact immutable
enrolled statement and original two direct dependencies, but two long unary
rewrite sequences from its preserved historical tactic script are replaced in
the newly constructed certificate by the existing, independently
kernel-checked `norm_num` tactic. Its authoring-only AST depth is bounded to
192; the resulting proof has 1,594 structural nodes, 1,594 proof objects, and
depth 145, with a canonical encoding envelope of 206 below the unchanged
limit of 256. The historical source script is not rewritten, and no kernel,
proof-codec, layered-replay, or 16/125,000/25,000 resource limit is raised.

The complete strict Bertrand root additionally uses the existing conservative
`intern_layered_replay_bodies` prepass: structurally equal immutable proof
subgraphs are shared without introducing a proof constructor, changing any
statement, or granting authority from object identity. All **544** transformed
dependency-curried proof bodies are independently checked again by the
unchanged intuitionistic kernel. The actual ordinary empty-context proof then
contains **201,285 structural proof nodes**, only **45,254 distinct proof
objects**, proof depth **235**, annotation size **1,299,972**, combined
envelope depth **244**, and **45 balanced layers**. It satisfies every unchanged
default layered bound, including 500,000 structural nodes, 100,000 proof
objects, 5,000,000 annotations, and depth 256; the original kernel explicitly
accepts the final exact `bertrand_strict` formula from the empty context.

## Independent release gates

```text
make peano-library-alpha-v18
make peano-library-alpha-v18-check
```

Generation and verification must reject changed parent bytes, mutated Stable
pointers, reordered enrollment, missing or altered proof data, false
dependency edges, wrong formulas, invalid ordinary proof bodies, unsupported
kernel modes, inaccurate bundle ownership, omitted flagship roots, and any
unrelated checked-use promotion. All historical Alpha-v1–v17 and Stable
artifacts remain reproducible byte-for-byte.
