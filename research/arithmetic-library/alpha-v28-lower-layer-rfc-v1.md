## Mathematical boundaries

G001 constructs both quotient and strictly bounded remainder for every
nonzero natural divisor and proves literal uniqueness of the pair. G002
constructs a gcd and actual signed-natural Bezout coefficient codes for
every pair, including `(0,0)`. The gcd is unique; Bezout coefficients are
not claimed unique. G003 constructs the quotient witnessing `a|c` from
`Coprime(a,b)` and `a|b*c`, with no extra positivity premise.

G004 constructs an actual beta-coded prime list and product trace for every
positive natural. The existing sorted construction supplies witnesses, but
sorting is neither a premise nor part of the new unordered relation. The
unit has the empty factor list; zero is not assigned a prime factorization.

G005 compares arbitrary unordered prime factorizations. It constructs a
beta-coded index permutation with boundedness, injectivity, surjectivity,
equal lengths, and entry-by-entry matching. Repeated primes are allowed.
The proof inducts on list length, locates and swaps a matching prime,
cancels its nonzero value, and restores a genuine matching bijection.
Literal uniqueness of raw beta codes and sorted-list equality are not
substitutes for this statement. The combined endpoint first constructs a
factor list and then constructs a permutation to every competing list.

G021 constructs a prime above every supplied natural bound. G022 constructs
exactly the first `k` primes, not a sparse Bertrand chain. Every transition
is to the genuinely least larger prime. Separate checked semantic roots
give primality of every entry, strict ordering, and no omitted smaller
prime. For positive `k`, the effective bound constructs the list, its last
index and value, and both powers establishing `p_k < 2^(2^k)`. The two
power witnesses are conclusions, not supplied inputs. The total list
existence theorem also includes the empty list at `k=0`.

The G081/G084 endpoints construct actual quotient and remainder witnesses
in the Gaussian/Eisenstein integer rings and a strictly smaller remainder
norm for every nonzero divisor. Both raw-coordinate roots accept arbitrary
integer differences, with only a nonzero-divisor premise. The coded roots
accept genuine canonical signed-pair codes and construct the quotient,
remainder, and both actual norms. Code validity is the carrier predicate,
not an extra arithmetic assumption; not every natural is a pair code.

The carrier and addition graph are shared. Gaussian multiplication and norm
are `(ac-bd,ad+bc)` and `a*a+b*b`. Eisenstein multiplication and norm are
`(ac-bd,ad+bc-bd)` and `a*a-a*b+b*b`. All subtractions are represented by
ordinary natural-number balance equations. Gaussian division uses witnessed
nearest-coordinate rounding; the Eisenstein fundamental parallelogram
already provides strict norm decrease using floor quotients. Global
nearest-point optimality in the Eisenstein lattice is not asserted.

G082/G083 and G085/G086 remain separate: this checkpoint does not claim
quadratic-ring unique factorization, complete prime classification, a
completed gcd algorithm, normal forms, or unrelated classification results.
