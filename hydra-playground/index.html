Variables range over natural numbers. Quantify them with forall n m. or exists k.

S n
successor, n + 1
+   *   =   <=
arithmetic and equality
->   /\   \/   ~

Use parentheses to group formulas. Expand abbreviations such as T(n); custom function definitions are not supported. Write fixed powers as repeated multiplication, such as n*n*n; ^ is not a primitive operator. Numerals are limited to 0–64.
