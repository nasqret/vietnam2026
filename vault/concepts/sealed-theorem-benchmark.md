# Sealed theorem benchmark

A **sealed theorem benchmark** is held by an independent evaluation owner and
opened once after source, library, solvers, models, search budgets, seeds, and
metrics are frozen.

Its separation unit is a mathematical lineage, not a tactic row. Before row
generation, connected components are formed from proof dependencies,
equivalent or stronger reformulations, problem families, generator seeds, and
authorship. For each target, training and retrieval exclude the target,
variants, proof, certificate, traces, descendants, revealing capstones, and
any theorem whose proof uses a masked node.

This extends [[genealogy-safe-proof-data-split]] to run-time imports and
retrieval. After unlock there is no tuning or training. A failed headline gate
is reported without changing the set, budget, or `pass@k` convention.

For a future quadratic-reciprocity experiment, the statement must be sealed
before its formal proof or teacher sketch enters the library, and the whole
development lineage must be masked.
