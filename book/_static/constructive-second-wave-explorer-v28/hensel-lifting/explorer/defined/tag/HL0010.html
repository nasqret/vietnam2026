HL0010

beta_horner_prime_power_hensel_lift_exists_unique

A positive prime-power-level simple root at an unrestricted input has a unique canonical lift and an actual next-power witness; the theorem even permits every nonzero base.

Alpha v28 checked-use · first admitted v27 · independently kernel and Lean verified; not Stable

The derivative-nonzero criterion supplies no inverse or power witness: both are constructed. Roots may be arbitrary natural representatives of signed integer polynomials. Singular-root classification and p-adic completion are separate milestones.
