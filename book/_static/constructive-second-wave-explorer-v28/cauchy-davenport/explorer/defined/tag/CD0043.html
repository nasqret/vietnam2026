prime_cauchy_davenport_normalized_cover_bound

Every normalized nonempty prime-field pair has the sharp bound against every actual coded upper sumset.

Alpha v28 checked-use · first admitted v27 · independently kernel and Lean verified; not Stable

Sets are complete characteristic-bit codes with actual finite cardinality witnesses. The proof constructs translations and the sumset; no finite-choice oracle, supplied cardinality conclusion, or unproved polynomial-method premise is used.
