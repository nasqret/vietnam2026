totient_euler_product_iff

The independently defined actual unit count and complete prime-support Euler product are equivalent on precisely the positive domain.

Alpha v30 checked-use · first admitted v29 · independently kernel and Lean verified; not Stable

Phi counts the actual 0/1 coprimality bits on 0≤a<n. EulerProduct has no Phi assumption or conclusion hidden in its definition. The empty product and the residue zero give Phi(1,1). The theorem does not assert Phi at zero.

Exact theorem in conservative defined notation

∀ n. ∀ t. (Phi(n,t) → EulerProduct(n,t)) ∧ (EulerProduct(n,t) → Phi(n,t))
