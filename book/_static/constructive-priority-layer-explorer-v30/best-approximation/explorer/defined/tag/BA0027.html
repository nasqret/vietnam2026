cf_approximation_natural_error_as_signed

Every natural numerator, including zero, is an actual signed numerator with negative component zero.

Alpha v30 checked-use · first admitted v29 · independently kernel and Lean verified; not Stable

The initial 0/1 convergent is included: u is natural, not necessarily positive. Comparison denominators are strictly smaller and positive.
