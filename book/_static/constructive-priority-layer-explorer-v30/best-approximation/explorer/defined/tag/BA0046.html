continued_fraction_initial_zero_over_one

For every positive G071 fraction below one, the genuine initial convergent is 0/1. This checked theorem explicitly corrects the old planning-only numerator-positivity error.

Alpha v30 checked-use · first admitted v29 · independently kernel and Lean verified; not Stable

The initial 0/1 convergent is included: u is natural, not necessarily positive. Comparison denominators are strictly smaller and positive. Signed competitors are represented by an arbitrary difference rp−rn. Approximation inequalities are proved from the trace, never stored as assumptions in Convergent.
