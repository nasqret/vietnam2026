eisenstein_coordinate_norm_product

The genuine Eisenstein norm is multiplicative, from checked conjugation, commutativity and associativity, without a norm or polynomial oracle.

A floor quotient in the fundamental parallelogram already gives the required strict norm decrease; global nearest-point optimality is not asserted.
