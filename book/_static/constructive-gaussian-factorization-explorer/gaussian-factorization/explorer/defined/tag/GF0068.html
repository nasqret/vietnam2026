gaussian_nonzero_product_divisor_unit_cofactor

If a nonzero actual Gaussian product divides one factor, its other factor has a constructed inverse; no abstract domain axiom is assumed.

Alpha v30 checked-use · first admitted v30 · independently kernel and Lean verified; not Stable
