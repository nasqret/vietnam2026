horner_taylor_successor_identity

The successor Horner transition has an exact subtraction-free quadratic Taylor remainder.

Alpha v25 checked-use · first admitted v25 · independently kernel and Lean verified; not Stable

G095 remains OPEN. The exact arbitrary-polynomial quadratic Taylor remainder, unique bounded modular correction and actual one-step Hensel divisibility lift are independently proved. Canonical unrestricted-input root representatives, uniqueness of lifted roots and the full prime-power lifting theorem are not proved.

Exact theorem in conservative defined notation

∀ r. ∀ d. ∀ h. ∀ q. ∀ t. ∀ a. (r + h · d + h · h · q) · (t + h) + a = r · t + a + h · (d · t + r) + h · h · (q · (t + h) + d)
