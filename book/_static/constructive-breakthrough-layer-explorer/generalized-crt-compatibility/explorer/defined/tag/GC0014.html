GC0014

crt_gcd_monotone_under_divisibility

A divisibility relation between inputs transports monotonically to their relational gcd values with any fixed natural, including zero.

Alpha v25 checked-use · first admitted v25 · independently kernel and Lean verified; not Stable

G011 remains OPEN. Every positive genuinely noncoprime finite list satisfying the exact successive-merge compatibility invariant has a proved unique canonical solution; arbitrary pairwise-compatible lists are proved only in the dominating-last case. The unrestricted pairwise-to-merge gcd/LCM distributivity bridge is not proved.
