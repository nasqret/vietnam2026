gaussian_norm_functional

The actual canonical Gaussian squared norm is unique across all arbitrary signed representatives.

Alpha v30 checked-use · first admitted v28 · independently kernel and Lean verified; not Stable

The natural-code carrier consists of genuine pairs of the existing signed integers; no new primitive arithmetic is trusted. The theorem constructs quotient, remainder, and actual norm witnesses. Gaussian gcd, unique factorization, and prime classification are separate targets.

Exact theorem in conservative defined notation

∀ z. ∀ N. ∀ M. GNorm(z,N) → GNorm(z,M) → N = M

Every linked abbreviation expands hygienically to the identical original native formula.
