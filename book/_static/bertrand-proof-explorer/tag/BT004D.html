bezout_mod_right

A balanced Bezout identity selects the left coefficient modulo the right modulus.

Exact expanded PA statement

forall m n xp yp xn yn. m * xp + n * yp = 1 + (m * xn + n * yn) -> exists u v. m * xp + n * u = (1 + m * xn) + n * v
