divisor_le_nonzero

Stable checked-use theorem · independently kernel verified

A divisor of a nonzero natural is bounded by that natural.

Exact expanded PA statement

forall d n. ~(n = 0) -> (exists q. n = d * q) -> exists k. k + d = n
