eisenstein_norm_functional

The canonical natural Eisenstein norm is unique, independently of all signed-coordinate representatives.

Alpha v32 checked-use · first admitted v28 · independently kernel and Lean verified; not Stable

Current library: Alpha v32, 3,971 checked-use theorems; Stable remains 432. Historical first admissions, original proof editions, and non-admitted aliases are preserved. Exact original first-admission records.

A floor quotient in the fundamental parallelogram already gives the required strict norm decrease; global nearest-point optimality is not asserted. The shared carrier is identical to the Gaussian carrier, but the multiplication law and norm are different. Eisenstein gcd, factorization, and prime classification remain separate targets.
