HL000A

beta_horner_simple_root_hensel_lift_exists_unique

Every unrestricted natural-polynomial simple root has a unique canonical lift, with no supplied correction or representative bound.

Alpha v29 checked-use · first admitted v27 · independently kernel and Lean verified; not Stable

The derivative-nonzero criterion supplies no inverse or power witness: both are constructed. Roots may be arbitrary natural representatives of signed integer polynomials. Singular-root classification and p-adic completion are separate milestones.
