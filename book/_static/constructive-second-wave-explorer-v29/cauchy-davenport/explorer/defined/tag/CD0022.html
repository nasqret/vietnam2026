CD0022

finite_modular_set_pullback_exists

Construct the genuine modular pullback set and prove its exact cardinality is unchanged by the finite permutation.

Alpha v29 checked-use · first admitted v27 · independently kernel and Lean verified; not Stable

Sets are complete characteristic-bit codes with actual finite cardinality witnesses. The proof constructs translations and the sumset; no finite-choice oracle, supplied cardinality conclusion, or unproved polynomial-method premise is used.

Exact theorem in conservative defined notation

∀ b. ∀ c. ∀ p. ∀ n. ∀ t. ¬p = 0 → BitCount(b,c,p,n) → ∃ x. ∃ y. BitCount(x,y,p,n) ∧ ModularSetPullback(b,c,x,y,p,t)
