CN000C

cornacchia_signed_step_direct

One Euclidean step turns a positive/negative root-coefficient pair into the next positive coefficient congruence.

Alpha v29 checked-use · first admitted v27 · independently kernel and Lean verified; not Stable

The output equation is proved from the real first-stop execution; it is not a trace-definition assumption. This is the prime sum-of-two-squares Cornacchia theorem, not a general x²+d y² solver.
