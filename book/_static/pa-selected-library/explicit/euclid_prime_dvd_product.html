euclid_prime_dvd_product

A prime dividing a product divides at least one factor (Euclid's lemma).

forall p a b. (~(p = 1) /\ forall c d. p = c * d -> c = 1 \/ d = 1) -> (exists k. a * b = p * k) -> (exists u. a = p * u) \/ exists v. b = p * v
