multiple_mul_left

A left multiple of a multiple remains a multiple.

candidate display only This reading surface grants no kernel, review, freeze, training, retrieval, or evaluation authority.

Exact selected statement

forall a n m. (exists q. n = a * q) -> exists s. m * n = a * s

Canonical: ∀ x. ∀ y. ∀ z. (∃ n. y = x · n) → ∃ n. z · y = x · n
