multiple_decidable

Divisibility of natural numbers is constructively decidable, including the zero divisor case.

candidate display only This reading surface grants no kernel, review, freeze, training, retrieval, or evaluation authority.

Exact selected statement

forall d n. (exists q. n = d * q) \/ ~(exists q. n = d * q)

Canonical: ∀ x. ∀ y. (∃ z. y = x · z) ∨ ¬(∃ z. y = x · z)
