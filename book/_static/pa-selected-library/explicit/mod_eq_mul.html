mod_eq_mul

Balanced natural congruence respects multiplication.

candidate display only This reading surface grants no kernel, review, freeze, training, retrieval, or evaluation authority.

Exact selected statement

forall m a b c d. (exists u v. a + m * u = b + m * v) -> (exists r s. c + m * r = d + m * s) -> exists x y. (a * c) + m * x = (b * d) + m * y
