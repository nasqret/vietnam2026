mod_eq_mul_right

Balanced congruence is preserved by multiplication on the right.

candidate display only This reading surface grants no kernel, review, freeze, training, retrieval, or evaluation authority.

forall m a b c. (exists u v. a + m * u = b + m * v) -> exists r s. (a * c) + m * r = (b * c) + m * s
