mod_eq_mul_left

Balanced congruence is preserved by multiplication on the left.

candidate display only This reading surface grants no kernel, review, freeze, training, retrieval, or evaluation authority.

Exact selected statement

forall m a b c. (exists u v. a + m * u = b + m * v) -> exists r s. (c * a) + m * r = (c * b) + m * s

Canonical: ∀ x. ∀ y. ∀ z. ∀ n. (∃ m. ∃ k. y + x · m = z + x · k) → ∃ m. ∃ k. n · y + x · m = n · z + x · k
