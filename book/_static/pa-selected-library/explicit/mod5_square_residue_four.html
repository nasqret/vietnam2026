mod5_square_residue_four

Squaring residue four modulo five gives residue one.

candidate display only This reading surface grants no kernel, review, freeze, training, retrieval, or evaluation authority.

Exact selected statement

forall z q. z = 5 * q + 4 -> z * z = 5 * ((q * z + 4 * q) + 3) + 1

Canonical: ∀ x. ∀ y. x = 5 · y + 4 → x · x = 5 · (y · x + 4 · y + 3) + 1
