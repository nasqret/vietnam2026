PD0032 BoundedNonzeroInverse

Conservative reading notation

candidate display only This reading surface grants no kernel, review, freeze, training, retrieval, or evaluation authority.

Meaning

a has a nonzero inverse strictly below m.

Parameters: m, a

exists dp_inverse. (~(dp_inverse = 0) /\ ((exists dp_bound. dp_bound + S dp_inverse = m) /\ (exists dp_u dp_v. (a) * (dp_inverse) + m * dp_u = 1 + m * dp_v)))
