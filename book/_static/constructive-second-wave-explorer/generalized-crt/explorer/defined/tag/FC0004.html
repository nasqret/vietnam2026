FC0004

crt_gcd_coprime_cofactors

A nonzero gcd yields both exact natural cofactors and their constructive coprimality.

Alpha v27 checked-use · first admitted v27 · independently kernel and Lean verified; not Stable

All finite lists are included, even the empty list and zero moduli. A positive LCM gives x<M; at zero LCM congruence is exact equality and normalization deliberately does not require the impossible x<0.

Exact theorem in conservative defined notation

∀ a. ∀ b. ∀ g. ¬g = 0 → IsGCD(g,a,b) → ∃ x. ∃ y. a = g · x ∧ (b = g · y ∧ (∀ z. Dvd(z,x) → Dvd(z,y) → z = 1))
