Prime(p)

p is nonunit and every factorization of p has a unit factor.

Conservative notation; not a theorem, primitive, or axiom.

Definition in prerequisite notation

¬p = 1 ∧ (∀ x. ∀ y. p = x · y → x = 1 ∨ y = 1)
