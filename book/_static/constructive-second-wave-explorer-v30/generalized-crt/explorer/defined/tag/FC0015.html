crt_pairwise_compatible_prefix_merge_iff

Pairwise compatibility and the formerly stronger predecessor-LCM merge invariant are constructively equivalent for arbitrary finite natural lists.

Alpha v30 checked-use · first admitted v27 · independently kernel and Lean verified; not Stable

All finite lists are included, even the empty list and zero moduli. A positive LCM gives x<M; at zero LCM congruence is exact equality and normalization deliberately does not require the impossible x<0.
