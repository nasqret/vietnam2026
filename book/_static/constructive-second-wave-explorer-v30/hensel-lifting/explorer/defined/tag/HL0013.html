beta_horner_prime_power_iterated_lifts_exists_unique

From every positive initial prime-power exponent, arbitrary finite further lifting constructs the actual higher power and its unique canonical root while preserving the entire initial residue class.

Alpha v30 checked-use · first admitted v27 · independently kernel and Lean verified; not Stable

The derivative-nonzero criterion supplies no inverse or power witness: both are constructed. Roots may be arbitrary natural representatives of signed integer polynomials. Singular-root classification and p-adic completion are separate milestones.
