totient_euler_factor_prefix_drop_last

A prefix retains all actual prime, exponent, factor, and preceding-power witnesses.

Alpha v29 checked-use · first admitted v29 · independently kernel and Lean verified; not Stable

Phi counts the actual 0/1 coprimality bits on 0≤a<n. EulerProduct has no Phi assumption or conclusion hidden in its definition. The empty product and the residue zero give Phi(1,1). The theorem does not assert Phi at zero.
