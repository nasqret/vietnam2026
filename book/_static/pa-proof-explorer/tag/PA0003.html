prime_divisor_eq_one_or_self

public native theorem

Every divisor of a prime is one or the prime itself.

Exact expanded PA statement

forall p g. (~(p = 1) /\ forall c d. p = c * d -> c = 1 \/ d = 1) -> (exists x. p = g * x) -> g = 1 \/ p = g
