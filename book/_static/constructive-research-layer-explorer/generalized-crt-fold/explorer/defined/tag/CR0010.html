CR0010

crt_prefix_lcm_exists_unique

Every arbitrary finite decoded modulus list, including noncoprime and zero entries, has a unique exact lcm.

Alpha v24 checked-use · first admitted v24 · independently kernel and Lean verified; not Stable

G011 remains OPEN. Every positive pairwise-coprime finite modulus list has a proved unique canonical CRT solution, and arbitrary finite lists have an exact LCM. The strictly stronger extension to arbitrary noncoprime pairwise gcd-compatible lists requires an unproved compatibility bridge and is not claimed.

∀ b. ∀ c. ∀ l. ∃ x. CRTPrefixLCM(b,c,l,x) ∧ (∀ y. CRTPrefixLCM(b,c,l,y) → y = x)
