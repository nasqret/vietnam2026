crt_prefix_lcm_successor_intro

Binary relational lcm extends the exact universal-property lcm of any finite decoded modulus prefix.

Alpha v25 checked-use · first admitted v24 · independently kernel and Lean verified; not Stable

G011 remains OPEN. Arbitrary finite positive noncoprime systems are solved under the exact predecessor-LCM merge invariant, and pairwise-compatible systems are solved when their final modulus dominates predecessors. Deriving the merge invariant from unrestricted pairwise gcd compatibility remains open.
