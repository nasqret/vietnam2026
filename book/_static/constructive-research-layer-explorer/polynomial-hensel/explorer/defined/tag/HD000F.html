beta_horner_derivative_linear

A genuine two-coefficient polynomial a*t+k has the exact decoded formal derivative a.

Alpha v24 checked-use · first admitted v24 · independently kernel and Lean verified; not Stable

G095 remains OPEN. Arbitrary beta-coded natural polynomial values and their unique exact formal derivatives are independently proved, but the necessary prime-precision Taylor congruence and modular simple-root Hensel lift have not been proved.

∀ b. ∀ c. ∀ t. ∀ n. ∀ z. HornerDerivative(b,c,t,2,n,z) → ∃ x. ∃ y. Beta(b,c,0,x) ∧ (Beta(b,c,1,y) ∧ (n = x · t + y ∧ z = x))
