## Mathematical continuation

1. Prove actual triangular convolution-prefix invariance and the append
   coefficient identity. For divisor length `S d`, coefficient `B_0 != 0`,
   input length `L`, and quotient length `max(L-d,0)`, each quotient entry
   is constructed using the actual prior convolution coefficient, field
   subtraction and the actual inverse of `B_0`.
2. Prove actual quotient construction, coefficient recovery, remainder
   existence and extensional uniqueness; use the checked subtraction and
   trimming graphs to obtain an empty remainder or reduced length at most
   `d`. A final product identity is a theorem, not a premise disguised as a
   definition. Cover short inputs, zero inputs and constant divisors.
3. Supply the needed representation/algebra bridge: harmless left padding,
   coefficient-by-power equality, trim invariance and aligned actual
   operations. Existing right padding is multiplication by a power of `X`,
   not an equality-preserving convention.
4. Prove polynomial gcd and actual Bézout witnesses by induction on the
   second reduced length, using genuine Euclidean division and actual
   associativity/distributivity. The backward step constructs
   `G = V*A + (U-V*Q)*B` from `A=B*Q+R` and `G=U*B+V*R`.
   The gcd of two zeros is zero; otherwise use a monic representative.
   Gcd uniqueness is coefficientwise, never raw-code identity. Bézout
   coefficients are not claimed unique.
5. Run actual dependency-closed HA/Lean checks, selected ordinary proofs,
   novelty and conservative-definition/reader regressions before claiming
   new research completion or admitting further entries.
