## Parallel proof tracks

1. **Actual divisor sums.** Prove signed-table prefix extension and append;
   construct Möbius-value tables and actual divisor masks. Define a divisor
   sum from the genuine `S n`-entry signed fold of the mask and prove totality
   and uniqueness for `0<n<=N`. The zero index contributes zero even when
   the input table has an arbitrary value at zero. Positive divisors and
   quotient witnesses are explicit. The Möbius function itself stays
   positive-domain only; no divisor cancellation is built into its definition.
2. **Signed weighted-sum algebra.** Construct pointwise sum/product and scalar
   tables using actual signed operations. Prove extensionality, sum linearity
   and scalar distributivity. Extend to actual rectangular row/column sums
   and finite Fubini when all required table constructors are proved, including
   empty dimensions. Reuse the first track's generic append operation rather
   than duplicating it under another name.
3. **Prime-field polynomial substrate.** Construct finite canonical coefficient
   beta tables, normalization, pointwise operations and modular Horner
   evaluation. State the coefficient order explicitly and reuse existing
   evaluation and residue graphs. Prove existence, uniqueness of represented
   values and re-encoding transport, without equating arbitrary encodings.
   Empty/zero polynomials and prime two are included. Polynomial division,
   gcd, irreducibles and general extension fields require separate proofs.

The subsequent G007 steps remain the actual prime-toggle involution and
divisor cancellation, weighted convolution laws and inversion on every
positive quotient in the input window. They must not be silently inferred
from the finite-sum foundations. Full G091 and G007 remain open until their
