## Mathematical continuation

The first route to try is induction on the rightmost factor's represented
length, not a claim that generic natural Fubini is necessary.

1. Actual trailing-zero/X-shift covariance. A trailing zero represents
   multiplication by X, unlike harmless leading-zero padding. Include empty
   factors and prove an actual operation relation, not raw beta-code equality.
2. Actual right-input scalar covariance using existing bilinearity or a
   one-dimensional natural scalar-sum law. Construct genuine intermediate
   products/sums and retain canonical coefficient guards.
3. An all-index append recurrence, with actual aligned additions and padding
   witnesses: `A*append(C,c) ≃ X*(A*C) + c*A`.
4. Product-length bookkeeping and convolution associativity by induction,
   including empty factors and constants. Horner/evaluation equality cannot
   replace formal coefficient identity over finite fields.
5. Actual right-oriented divisibility and linear-combination witnesses,
   Euclidean backward transport, then degree descent for gcd/Bézout. Preserve
   `G = V*A + (U-V*Q)*B`, the zero/zero case and monic normalization; do not
   claim uniqueness of Bézout coefficients.

Each new tranche gets conservative definition audits, actual conditional HA,
independent contracts/models/rejection tests, dependency-complete HA/Lean
and bounded ordinary replay before being reported as proved. Proposed or
partially implemented bridges are not counted as completed milestones.
